About me

Hi! I’m Ana Pérez Asensio, a 22-year-old audiovisual designer and illustrator with a passion for branding, video editing and visual storytelling. I’m drawn to the process of turning ideas into visual concepts, building identities with character and creating images that communicate beyond words.

I enjoy experimenting with analog and digital techniques, mixing different materials, textures and tools to discover unexpected results. Always curious and eager to learn, I’m inspired by new creative approaches and love bringing a personal touch to every project I work on.
