Roccos is a branding project for a traditional Italian gelateria, blending the authenticity of Italian ice cream culture with a fresh, contemporary aesthetic. The visual identity brings together timeless references and modern design to create a distinctive and cohesive brand experience across packaging, menus, loyalty cards and promotional videos.
